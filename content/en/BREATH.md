# Breath

The fundamental unit of Shiftopia is the **Breath**: 150 milliseconds of conscious pause.  
This interval is the boundary between "automatic response" and "real presence". Every breath taken is recorded in the ledger.

## Expected Breath vs. Real Breath
- **Expected Breath:** Those taken out of habit, obligation, or mere compliance with a rule.
- **Real Breath:** Those accompanied by "choice", where you could have said "no" but said "yes" from the depths (or vice versa).

The civilization of Shiftopia is built on real breaths. **Strain** is the difference between the two.

## Recording a Real Breath
Each real breath must be recorded in the ledger. This is done by opening an Issue with the label `breath-real` and explaining "what made this breath different".  
Three witnesses (at least Stream layer) must attest that the breath was real.

> "Breathing cannot be faked. Those present know."
