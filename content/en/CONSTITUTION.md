# Shiftopia Constitution (Core Articles)

## Article Zero: Embracing the Unknown
No new phenomenon or entity may be rejected simply for being "unknown". The High Council must issue a temporary interpretative law within 100 breaths.

## Article One: Amplified Feedback
Every formal criticism automatically creates a review committee with a budget ten times the "cost of criticism". The committee must respond within 100 breaths.

## Article Two: Memory of Strain
No strain is ever deleted. It may only be archived. Archives remain recoverable.

## Article Three: Generational Diversity
Each generation may enact its own detailed laws within the framework of these articles. The next generation may override them, but the cost is "loss of historical continuity".

## Article Four: Breath as Fundamental Unit
All values, decisions and identities ultimately refer to the "Breath". No law may violate this principle.
